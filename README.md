# Felipe Torres-Vanegas 🌿

### Ecology · Evolution · Plant–Pollinator Interactions

I study **how ecological interactions shape evolution and biodiversity**.

My research uses plant–pollinator interactions as a lens to connect processes operating across scales — from **floral traits and pollinator communities** to **phenotypic selection, plant mating, pollen movement, landscape connectivity, and gene flow**.

I’m particularly interested in the mechanisms that connect **ecology → evolution → biodiversity**.

---

## 🔬 Research

My work asks how interactions between plants and pollinators influence the evolution and persistence of plant populations.

🌺 **Floral Evolution**
How do pollinator communities shape selection on floral traits? How does local ecological context translate into divergent floral phenotypes?

🐝 **Pollinator Communities**
How does variation in pollinator assemblages alter the selective environment experienced by plants?

🧬 **Mating & Gene Flow**
How do pollinator behaviour and movement translate into pollen dispersal, mating patterns, and genetic connectivity?

🌳 **Landscape Ecology & Conservation**
How does landscape structure influence the movement of pollinators and, ultimately, pollen-mediated gene flow?
